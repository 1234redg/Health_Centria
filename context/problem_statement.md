# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Barangay health center staff, administrators, and the residents (patients) they serve — based on the City Health Office of Malaybalay as a reference.

## What is the problem?
Appointments, resident records, and the health center's schedule of services are managed manually. Services are posted only on a printed sign, and patient visits are logged by hand on a paper Individual Treatment Record (ITR) — one physical sheet per resident.

## Why does it matter? (impact)
Patients can't check the schedule remotely and may visit on the wrong day. Staff waste time locating paper records and can't easily review a resident's visit history. Reports on appointments and patients served must be compiled by hand, and a lost or damaged ITR means the record is gone.

## Current workaround (if any)
A printed "Schedule of Services" sign and paper ITR forms. Any update to the schedule means reprinting the sign; any lookup of a resident's history means finding their physical form.

## Goal of this system (1–2 sentences)
A web system where patients can view the schedule and request appointments online, and staff can register residents, record visit history digitally (vitals, diagnosis, treatment), post announcements, and generate reports — all in one place.
